## 1. Problem

**Confirmed — Operational problem statement**

Today, PDCA is primarily documented in Excel. One problem may contain many Facts, Assumptions/Hypotheses, Actions, Results, dates, and PICs, with numerous bullet points stored in single cells on the same row. For example, one problem can have 10 facts, 5 hypotheses, and 20 actions with different relationships. Excel's row/cell presentation makes the many-to-many reasoning and work relationships hard to see and maintain.

Consequences:
- Difficult to establish which fact supports which hypothesis, which action tests which hypothesis, and which result belongs to which action.
- Difficult to trace reasoning **backward** from a result to the action, hypothesis, facts, and original problem.
- Individual actions, PICs, dates, and outcomes are hard to follow when recorded inside long multi-item cells.
- As PDCA cases grow, reading, communicating, tracking, and carrying learning forward becomes difficult.

**Problem to solve:** Structure PDCA as individually traceable, freely linkable information rather than many unrelated bullet items inside a spreadsheet row.

## 2. Desired Outcome / Success Criteria

**Confirmed — Desired outcome**

A single, easy-to-use **factory-wide web-based PDCA platform** with one common method across departments. Users can connect Problem → Fact → Assumption/Hypothesis → Action → Status & Result → Measures to be implemented freely (many-to-many), including backward reasoning, images, document links, action follow-up, centralized persistent records, and simultaneous collaboration.

**Success criteria — mixed confirmation:** SC-04 numerical thresholds are **Confirmed** (D-010/D-012/D-054); SC-01–SC-03 and SC-05–SC-08 describe expected outcomes/evidence to validate during Pilot. No extra quantitative value/time-savings gates have been approved.

- **SC-01 — Traceability:** A user can follow a Result back through its related Action(s), Hypothesis/Hypotheses, Fact(s), and Problem where those links have been entered.
- **SC-02 — Item-level tracking:** Users can distinguish individual Actions and their PIC, dates, and status/result without reading an entire Excel-style cell.
- **SC-03 — Same standard:** Pilot users follow the same seven categories regardless of their department.
- **SC-04 — Ease of use (confirmed D-010, D-012):** At least **80% of first-time pilot users overall AND at least 80% in EACH participating department** complete agreed essential tasks **without assistance**, and the **overall average ease-of-use rating is at least 4/5**, using a standard web browser. Department-specific satisfaction thresholds have not been agreed.
- **SC-05 — Visual evidence:** Users can associate images and document references with the applicable PDCA content.
- **SC-06 — Central record:** Authorized factory personnel can retrieve the same saved PDCA from separate devices/sessions.
- **SC-07 — Collaboration:** Multiple authorized users can view and edit the same PDCA, without unreported loss of one another's changes.
- **SC-08 — Pilot-to-scale:** A pilot provides evidence of value and material issues before expansion to the wider factory.

**Confirmed — Pilot success policy: Functional Excellence + Practical Usability (Decision D-009).** Expansion requires every *agreed mandatory functional/data/safety gate* to PASS and the *agreed ease-of-use criteria* to be met. Minor non-blocking defects may be carried forward only when documented and explicitly accepted by the project owner; critical failures, silent loss of work, or unresolved issues that prevent core PDCA tasks cannot count as a PASS. A functional demo alone is insufficient.

**Confirmed — Usability thresholds and aggregation (D-010, D-011, D-012):** First-time users' independent essential-task completion must be **≥80% overall AND ≥80% within every participating department**. Mean ease-of-use rating must be **≥4/5 overall**; no separate department-level rating threshold has been approved. At least **5 first-time pilot testers per department** are required. The per-person success rule is now confirmed (D-054): **100% of designated critical tasks plus ≥80% of all standard tasks**, without assistance. Critical-task *types* are now confirmed (D-055): create/edit/link Node, successful Auto-save, and trace from Result back to Problem. Exact checklist, scripts, survey wording and complete Pilot protocol still require approval. Timing and efficiency improvements versus Excel are separate proposed measures; no time-savings target is approved.

**Unknown:** Exact standardized task checklist and scripts for the agreed critical activities (D-055), survey wording/scale, final participant count above the minimum, detailed Hard Gate evidence and remaining operational execution details for plant-wide rollout. Official Pilot duration is three working days (D-056), with one real case per department (D-057). Participant scoring (D-054), usability thresholds and overall/per-department aggregation (D-010–D-012) are confirmed. Final GO authority, failure retest discretion, voluntary rollout and no project-level PDCA owner are confirmed (D-070–D-073).

## 3. Users / Stakeholders

- **Confirmed — Eligible users:** Internal factory personnel only.
- **Confirmed — Pilot group:** Cutting & Packing plus **1–2 additional factory departments** before wider rollout. The other departments and participants are not yet selected.
- **Confirmed — Pilot usability sample (D-011):** At least **5 first-time users per department**. A two-department pilot therefore requires at least 10 testers; a three-department pilot at least 15. Testers should represent actual intended internal users. This is a minimum, not a maximum or a statistical representativeness claim.
- **Confirmed — Collaboration:** Any authorized user can view, create, and edit PDCA records of any department; no department-specific edit restriction.
- **Confirmed — PIC / Owner field:** Indicates responsibility for an activity; does not grant exclusive editing rights.
- **Confirmed — Pilot display-name identification (D-032):** Participants type their own display name without a password or Microsoft Account. The name is displayed during collaboration and in revision history, but it is self-declared, not verified identity. Internal-only access must be enforced separately.
- **Confirmed — Scope is one PDCA at a time for Node linking (D-041):** Users may connect Nodes freely only when both Nodes belong to the same PDCA. No direct links between separate PDCA projects in the initial scope.
- **Confirmed — Pilot device baseline (D-052):** Desktop web browsers only, targeting Full HD **1920 × 1080** pixels at **16:9**; Mobile and Tablet interfaces are excluded from the initial Pilot scope.
- **Confirmed — Performance case (D-058):** Validate approximately 200 Nodes per PDCA (not a hard product maximum), including their relationships, while roughly ten users can work on the same PDCA concurrently (D-024/D-025).
- **Confirmed — Directed PDCA links (D-060):** Within one PDCA, each Node link has a source and target. An arrow indicates direction, and users can inspect/traverse links in either direction for reasoning. Link-type taxonomy is not required for Pilot.
- **Confirmed — Link change history (D-061):** Track creation/removal of links, with the participant's entered display name and timestamp, and allow restoration of mistakenly removed links, respecting currently existing Node endpoints and current graph rules. Names are self-declared, not authenticated (D-032).
- **Confirmed — Circular relationships (D-062):** Link cycles are permitted without a special warning, and graph traversal must avoid infinite looping.
- **Confirmed — Concurrent link editing (D-063):** Multiple users can add/remove links in the same PDCA simultaneously; conflicts must not silently discard another user's link changes, and completed updates propagate automatically to collaborators.
- **Confirmed — Unique directed links (D-064):** At most one active directed link from a given source Node A to target Node B (A → B); the reverse link (B → A) is a distinct allowed relationship. Creating duplicate A → B must not produce a second active link, including on concurrent creation.
- **Confirmed — Pilot mandatory functional/data-safety gates (D-065):** Official Pilot must demonstrate *all accepted in-scope functionality* works, correct Node/link persistence, no silent lost or overwritten data under concurrent work, verified backup/restore of the full saved dataset, and access restricted to authorized internal factory users. These gates are mandatory in addition to existing novice usability rules (D-009–D-012, D-054). The ~200-Node/10-editor scenario remains a required workload check (D-058), but **no separate numerical performance threshold is an additional Hard Gate** at this time; specific acceptance test scripts remain to be finalized.
- **Confirmed — Factory LAN access boundary (D-066):** For initial Pilot, restrict access to authorized factory desktop computers connected through the *internal factory LAN*. Do not provide direct Internet access or Company VPN remote access in Pilot. Being on the LAN by itself must **not** grant unrestricted access; the system must apply appropriate independent network/host authorization controls, since display names are not authenticated (D-032). Concrete controls and IT permissions are deferred.
- **Confirmed — Primary and backup system administrators (D-067):** Assign a primary administrator and at least one alternate/backup administrator for Host operation, backup, recovery and application maintenance. **This explicitly supersedes the earlier tentative selection of IT as sole administrator**; IT may advise/support and its security/hosting approval remains necessary, but is not automatically assigned sole operational ownership. Names, roles, handover process and IT coordination are pending.
- **Confirmed — Four-hour RPO (D-068):** Set **Recovery Point Objective ≤4 hours** for a recoverable system failure when a valid, accessible backup remains available. The automated on-Host backup schedule and consistency safeguards must be designed and checked to meet this recovery-loss bound. This **cannot guarantee recovery** after loss of all Host storage and backups (D-046). Exact backup/retention implementation and testing are still pending.
- **Confirmed — One-working-day RTO (D-069):** Set **Recovery Time Objective ≤1 working day** for a recoverable service failure with usable backup and functioning/replacement infrastructure available. This is an objective to be validated against the actual primary/alternate admin availability, host constraints and restore method; it does **not** promise service recovery after total Host destruction without infrastructure or backup.
- **Confirmed — Final Pilot GO/REFINE/NO-GO authority (D-070):** The user as **Project Owner** personally makes the final decision whether to expand THE NODE OF PDCA after Pilot. The owner's GO decision must be grounded in passed Hard Gates and usability acceptance (D-009/D-065), and company-required IT/security/infrastructure approvals are separate prerequisites, not substituted by owner approval. No additional multi-party product GO committee is required.
- **Confirmed — Failure remediation and retesting policy (D-071):** If Pilot fails, the Project Owner decides case by case whether to rerun the complete three-working-day Pilot or perform targeted retests of affected functionality plus appropriate regression checks. **Failed, untested or invalidated mandatory Hard Gates cannot be waived or counted as passed**; evidence is required to confirm every impacted mandatory requirement again before GO.
- **Confirmed — Voluntary factory-wide adoption after approval (D-072):** Once Pilot has passed and the Project Owner declares GO, make the service available to **all authorized factory departments** without mandatory adoption, staged departmental rollout order or required adoption dates. Existing factory-LAN-only, internal-user and desktop restrictions remain in force until separately changed.
- **Confirmed — No required per-PDCA project Owner (D-073):** Creating, editing or continuing a PDCA does **not** require appointing a record/project-level Owner or project approval. All authorized internal factory users retain shared edit access (D-004). This does **not** remove action-level PIC/assigned responsibility fields (R-03), the system administrators (D-067), or the overall Project Owner (D-070).
- **Confirmed — No inactivity-based archive or deletion (D-074):** Retain and make inactive PDCA records searchable/accessible indefinitely while the applicable data and Host remain available, **without automatic Archive or inactivity-triggered deletion/reminders**. A PDCA remains editable as before (D-043). Explicit user-initiated deletion follows the existing seven-day PDCA Trash/automatic purge rules (D-044), not an idle-time policy.
- **Confirmed — Empty PDCA records permitted (D-075):** Users may create and save an entirely empty PDCA, with no Problem Node or any other Node required at creation or save time. Nodes and relationships may be added later; the existing no-Template/no-PDCA-Duplicate rule (D-048) still applies.
- **Confirmed — Free Node reclassification (D-076):** An existing Node may change freely among the seven fixed Node categories, without a mandatory confirmation dialog, a replacement Node, loss of its contents, images, document references, editing history, or existing links. The change must respect the existing same-Node edit protection and automatic persistence, with other viewers seeing the saved category.
- **Confirmed — Standalone/unconnected Nodes (D-077):** A Node may be created and kept with zero links, indefinitely, without warnings or forced linking. The user may attach, revise or remove its links at any time within the original PDCA.
- **Confirmed — No cross-PDCA Node transfer or duplication (D-078):** Initial scope includes **neither moving nor copying a Node between PDCA projects**. To reuse such material, a user manually creates a new Node in the other PDCA; no automatic cross-PDCA relationship or history transfer occurs. Existing within-PDCA link boundaries (D-041) remain unchanged.
- **Confirmed — Node category-change audit trail and revert (D-079):** Save category-change events with the previous category, next category, self-entered display name and timestamp. Users may revert to a formerly recorded category without losing the Node's current content, attachments or existing edges. Reverting is itself recorded as a new change, preserving earlier events. The display name is not authenticated (D-032). Category change history is confirmed in addition to existing Node text versioning (D-033/D-034); specific history storage/retention mechanics remain DESIGN/data-governance details.
- **Confirmed — Search across PDCA titles and Node text (D-080):** Provide one simple search that finds both matching PDCA titles and matching textual Node contents across the PDCA records the user may access. Results identify the containing PDCA and matching Node where relevant, and let the user open that result in the application without requiring advanced search filters.
- **Confirmed — No sharing/permalink feature (D-081):** Do not implement a dedicated Copy Link/Share PDCA/Share Node workflow or a Node-specific deep-link route in the initial scope. Users find and open PDCA/Node information through the application's primary search/navigation. This does not forbid ordinary internal hyperlinks inside Node content to other documents (D-022/D-023) or standard browser navigation where technically unavoidable.
- **Confirmed — Required name but duplicate PDCA titles allowed (D-082):** Every saved PDCA must have a non-empty display name/title. Multiple separate PDCAs may use the **same name**; assign each PDCA a persistent **unique internal ID** so their Nodes, links, images and history cannot be mixed up. A blank PDCA remains allowed (D-075) in the sense of **zero Nodes**, not a missing title.
- **Confirmed — PDCA title can be freely edited (D-083):** Any authorized PDCA editor may change its name at any time without requiring a special confirmation or mandatory rename history. Renaming must not change its internal unique ID or damage/alter its Nodes, attachments, relationships or stored records. A separate rename audit trail is not required by this decision.
- **Confirmed — No department field/group on PDCA (D-084):** PDCA records are part of one shared factory-wide collection and **do not require or expose a PDCA-department assignment, department list or department-based grouping** for Pilot. Editing access stays independent of department (D-004); department affiliation of **Pilot participants and real-work test scenarios** remains recorded in the test protocol for the confirmed per-department success checks (D-011/D-012/D-057). No automatic linkage from test participant's department to PDCA metadata is implied.
- **Confirmed — Optional structured Action PIC and Due Date (D-085):** Each Action Node provides distinct PIC and Due Date fields for tracking, **both optional**; an Action can be created/saved with text alone and assigned PIC/Due Date later. They remain separate from the main Node text and do not establish exclusive edit rights. No new required validation beyond the general Node requirements is implied.
- **Confirmed — Built-in Action Status (D-086):** Each Action Node has its own editable, structured Status field for tracking, independent of the existing **Status & Result** Node category. Changing Action Status does not require creating or connecting a Status & Result Node, which may still capture results/evidence separately.
- **Confirmed — Exactly five standard Action Status values (D-087, default D-093):** The fixed set is **Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Canceled, Blocked**. Canceled is distinct from Blocked; Blocked means an obstacle remains and is not equivalent to completion or cancellation. Users select from these five values rather than typing arbitrary statuses. **New Actions default to Not Started** (D-093); any forced transition order remains unapproved.
- **Confirmed — Completed without mandatory result (D-088):** A user may set an Action's status to **Completed** immediately, without needing to fill a result-summary field or create/connect a Status & Result Node or evidence. Users may record resulting observations and attachments later. This does not remove the requirement to preserve existing fields/data.
- **Confirmed — Action Status history for every change (D-089):** For each saved change of Action Status, store the preceding and next status, the participant's self-entered display name and the timestamp. Make status history reviewable. No dedicated one-click historical status restoration is required; users may update the current status normally and that new change is recorded. Entered names are not verified identities (D-032). Lifetime retention is confirmed by D-097; collision mechanics remain open.
- **Confirmed — Calculated Overdue indication (D-090):** For an Action with a Due Date that has passed, show Overdue when its current Status is anything **other than Completed or Canceled** (including Blocked). Overdue is a derived indication **not a sixth Action Status**, must not overwrite its existing Status, and must not create notifications/reminders. An Action with no Due Date cannot be Overdue; date-boundary details are specified by D-091.
- **Confirmed — Date-only Action deadline (D-091):** Due Date records a **calendar date only**, with no due time-of-day. It is due through the end of that date in the factory's local date context, and becomes overdue **from the following local calendar day** if still eligible under D-090. No separate time entry is required.
- **Confirmed — Per-PDCA Action Overview (D-092):** Provide a convenient Action Overview **inside each individual PDCA**, listing its own Actions with their Status, completion/open work, and Overdue indications. Do not add a factory-wide consolidated cross-PDCA Action dashboard in the initial scope. All authorized PDCA collaborators may view its Overview.
- **Confirmed — Default Action Status Not Started (D-093):** Each newly created Action Node begins with **Not Started** as its saved Status without requiring manual selection. Users may change it later among the five approved statuses (D-087).
- **Confirmed — One optional primary PIC name (D-094):** Action has **one** optional primary PIC, represented as a simple text field rather than a structured multi-person list. Additional contributors may be described in the ordinary Action text, without a mandatory co-owner field; this does not impose a PDCA-level Owner (D-073).
- **Confirmed — Due Date change history (D-095):** Whenever the Due Date is changed, record **old date, new date, the participant's self-entered display name, and timestamp**, including setting/clearing an optional date where applicable. Review past edits without requiring a reason for the change. This is in addition to Action Status change history (D-089). Display names are unverified (D-032); retention for the record lifetime is confirmed by D-097; technical implementation remains open.
- **Confirmed — Simple non-Action Nodes (D-096):** For the six Node categories other than Action, use common title/name and free-text content, with optional attached images and external document links; do not require type-specific structured fields. Action's distinct PIC, Due Date and Status rules remain.
- **Confirmed — History retained for record lifetime (D-097):** Retain approved Node text revisions, Node-category changes, directed-link create/delete events, Action Status changes and Action Due Date changes for as long as the owning Node/PDCA remains retained, including seven-day recoverable Trash. No automatic 1-/3-year history expiry. On final permanent deletion remove that record's live application history. Retained backup snapshots are subject to a separately confirmed 30-day backup retention policy (D-106), with IT handling of deleted records still open; no special image deletion audit is implied.
- **Confirmed — Images can be added/removed without separate audit (D-098):** Authorized editors may add or remove individual Node image attachments without separately recording a per-image add/remove audit event and without a dedicated image-level restore feature; other attached images, Node content, graph edges and already confirmed histories remain intact. Existing Node/PDCA Trash and whole-system backup still apply.
- **Confirmed — PDF + Excel + JSON exports (D-099):** Support the already approved hybrid graph-plus-details PDF report, structured tabular Excel export, and machine-readable JSON export for a PDCA. Markdown is not mandatory in initial scope. None of these formats substitutes for the automatic full-system Backup/Restore.
- **Confirmed — Export one selected PDCA only (D-100):** Each user-facing export contains exactly one selected PDCA's records; no multi-PDCA batch, combined export or factory-wide database export is required. The separate system-wide backup remains unchanged.
- **Confirmed — JSON includes current graph + retained histories; image references only (D-101):** For a single PDCA, JSON includes current PDCA identity/title, Node IDs/categories/text, Action PIC/Due Date/Status, directed edges, source-document hyperlinks and retained approved text/category/link/Action-status/Action-due-date histories. Image attachments appear only as references/metadata, not embedded binary images; the JSON may not provide accessible photos outside the host. No JSON Import, Restore or ZIP-with-images feature is approved. Schema and trashed-data inclusion remain future implementation questions.
- **Confirmed — Preserve hidden Action fields on category changes (D-102):** When a Node is reclassified from Action to another type, preserve but hide its Action-specific PIC, Due Date and Status. If reclassified back to Action, show those retained values, rather than reset or clear them. Preserve the existing Node ID, contents, images, document links, graph relationships and all agreed histories.
- **Confirmed — Excel export: Nodes and Relationships (D-103):** Per-PDCA Excel export has two worksheets: Nodes and Relationships. Nodes lists Node ID, category, text and applicable Action PIC/Due Date/Status; Relationships lists existing directed Source → Target Node links with stable IDs. History worksheets are not required in Excel; approved saved histories remain available in JSON (D-101).
- **Confirmed — Freely reopen Completed/Canceled Actions (D-104):** An Action in Completed or Canceled may be changed back to another of the five approved statuses (including Not Started, In Progress or Blocked) without a mandatory reason or approval. Log each change in existing Status History (D-089/D-097). Recompute Overdue based on Due Date and new current Status (D-090/D-091).
- **Confirmed — Disallow A-to-A self-link (D-105):** Do not allow a directed relationship from a Node to itself (A → A). Directed cycles involving two or more different Nodes remain allowed (A → B → A), following D-062. Apply to edge creation, recovery and saved/exported active graph structure while maintaining single-edge-per-ordered-pair rule (D-064).
- **Confirmed — Rolling thirty-day Host-only backup retention (D-106):** Maintain automated full-dataset backup snapshots on the Host for a rolling 30-day retention window and remove older snapshots according to the retention procedure. Preserve adequate and valid restore points to achieve the approved recoverable RPO ≤4 hours and RTO ≤1 working day (D-068/D-069). Validate Host storage capacity and backup integrity; failure must not be silent. Whole Host data/storage destruction remains an accepted unrecoverable risk (D-046). In-app Node/PDCA Trash recovery remains seven days, not 30 days; backup copies of purged data may survive within the 30-day backup window pending company IT erasure policy. The accepted cadence and Restore design are in §7. Verify capacity and the controls for live operation with IT.
- **Confirmed — Every authorized internal user may export (D-107):** Any independently authorized internal factory user who may access a PDCA can export that PDCA as PDF, Excel or JSON. No export-only administrator permission or file-format tier is required. User-entered display names are not authentication; the factory LAN/application access boundary and company information security obligations still control who may use the system. Export remains per single PDCA (D-100) with JSON image references only (D-101).
- **Intended future audience:** Personnel across the entire factory.
- **Unknown:** Which 1–2 additional departments participate, actual tester names/roles and any additional users beyond the 5-per-department minimum, definition of internal personnel for contractor cases, identity of required IT/security/infrastructure approvers, and formal GO evidence/report format. The overall Project Owner is already the sole final product GO decision-maker (D-070).

## 4. Constraints

**Confirmed**
- Browser-based web usage; ease of use for staff accustomed to everyday web applications.
- One common PDCA categorization and principle across departments.
- A central persistent record, image support, and simultaneous multi-user operation.
- Pilot-first rollout before opening use across the entire factory, starting with Cutting & Packing and 1–2 other departments.
- Authorized access limited to internal factory personnel; sharing does not imply public access.
- **Product delivery strategy (D-013):** Develop THE NODE OF PDCA as a purpose-built application for the factory's needs. **Do not purchase/adopt a new off-the-shelf PDCA/QMS/whiteboard product as the core solution.** Existing market products may inform benchmarking and UX/functional requirements, not replace the planned custom product.
- **Open-source policy (D-014, confirmed):** Existing open-source libraries and frameworks may be used to develop the custom application, subject to license compliance and compatibility with private internal company use. No specific library, framework, architecture, or license is approved yet.
- **Internal-only operation (D-014, confirmed):** The finished application is for authorized internal company use only, not public distribution or external customers. The deployment/access design must enforce that boundary; whether the system is hosted on-premises or in an approved private environment is not yet selected.
- **Host availability (D-015, clarified by D-029):** Temporary downtime while the host computer is switched off is acceptable. No always-on availability or automatic failover is required. Previously saved PDCA data must remain accessible after service resumes. For the Pilot, editing pauses during LAN/host disconnection and users are warned; unsaved draft preservation is separate (D-030), **not full offline editing**.
- **Host restart (D-016):** When the host computer is powered on again, the application must become available automatically, without an operator manually starting the application. The mechanism is deferred to DESIGN.
- **Interim host (D-018):** The owner intends to use their currently available laptop as a temporary host for initial use/testing while it is powered on, before the planned shared local server is ready. This is an approved host preference/operating requirement, **not** evidence that the application is installed or running, that LAN reachability or company IT authorization has been verified, or permission to begin DESIGN/BUILD.
- **Host transition/data portability (D-019):** When moving THE NODE OF PDCA from the interim laptop to the planned local server, **all previously saved project data must move with it**, including text-based PDCA records, node/link relationships, attached images and references to supporting documents where applicable. Previously saved content and connections must remain accessible and intact after the move. Exact storage technology, transfer method, validation procedure, and handling of externally hosted document references are deferred to DESIGN; no migration has yet been performed.
- **Visual evidence expectations (D-020):** Images are primarily staff screenshots/screen captures and real workplace photos. An operational estimate is around **50 images for a single Problem/PDCA case across its constituent nodes**, **not** 50 images per node; this is not a hard cap, and per-node attachment counts are unrestricted at the DEFINE stage. Automatic image compression is acceptable provided evidence remains legible and usable. Exact upload size, storage quota, image format, and compression settings remain unspecified.
- **Node text expectations (D-021):** Text content within an individual Node is normally **a few short paragraphs**, not an essay or multi-page report. Approximately **10,000 characters per Node** is the owner's suggested upper guideline, **not yet a confirmed hard validation limit**; the final enforcement behavior, character-count definition, and treatment of larger records remain open for DESIGN/DEFINE clarification.
- **Document references by hyperlink only (D-022, clarified by D-023):** Do **not** upload or store copies of non-image documents (e.g., PDF, Excel, Word) in THE NODE OF PDCA. Nodes may store references to original documents in existing factory storage or to other relevant online information/data sources. Images remain in scope as stored evidence per D-020. Moving hosts must preserve saved hyperlinks, **not migrate external source files**. Factory systems include Microsoft services and LAN network folders; external internet URLs are also legitimate references. Users will copy/paste links and take responsibility for checking whether references are valid. No automatic link verification, crawling, source integration, or guarantee that a local network-file path launches from a browser is required in Pilot. Clicking a supported link should attempt to open/navigate to its destination; source access remains subject to permissions and network/browser capabilities. Specific protocol compatibility is a DESIGN/IT implementation check, **not a DEFINE blocker**.
- **Shared PDCA collaboration workload (D-024, clarified by D-025):** Cutting & Packing has approximately **10 participants** who may join a table meeting to review **the same PDCA page/case at the same time**. **All 10 may open the same PDCA from their own separate computers and actively edit it simultaneously during the Pilot (D-025).** Changes must not silently overwrite or erase another participant's work; simultaneous-edit correctness must be verified in Pilot. This estimate is **per PDCA case in a meeting**, **not** a factory-wide simultaneous-user cap. Live feedback is specified by D-026; same-node edit contention is addressed by the approved Node-level temporary lock in D-027. No specific synchronization technology, performance SLA, or implementation is approved.
- **Automatic live collaboration feedback (D-026):** User expects an experience resembling the factory's Microsoft Excel for the web: while approximately 10 users work in the same PDCA, each user sees **which Node another participant is currently editing** (e.g., editor identity/visible editing state) and sees **completed changes appear automatically on their own screen without manually refreshing**. Show sufficiently prompt feedback to support a live shared meeting. This does **not** imply that every unfinished keystroke must be broadcast, nor establish a millisecond latency SLA. The behavior for two users attempting to edit the exact same Node is addressed by the user-approved temporary per-Node edit lock (D-027); no specific implementation/library is chosen.
- **Temporary Node-level edit protection (D-027):** For Pilot, multiple users may edit **different Nodes in one PDCA simultaneously**. When an authorized user begins editing a particular Node, only that user may change that Node until the edit session ends; others can still **view** it and see **who is editing** but cannot edit the same Node concurrently. On finishing/leaving the edit session, release the temporary restriction while respecting the automatic-saving state (D-028) and show completed changes to all open users automatically (D-026). If the editor disconnects or abandons the session, the lock must eventually be released without an indefinite block and without silently losing **saved** work. Unsaved draft preservation and recovery on reconnect are specified in D-030; exact Node-lock expiry/disconnect thresholds remain undecided. This is a **user-approved behavioral requirement**, not a choice of database, lock algorithm, or technology.
- **Excel-for-the-web-like automatic saving (D-028):** Edits to Node content/fields must be saved automatically during the editing workflow, without requiring a manual Save action. Users should be able to distinguish when changes are **saving**, **successfully saved**, or **not successfully saved**; failures or disconnection must not be falsely shown as saved. Once saved, changes become visible to other connected users automatically (D-026). Permit restoration to a previous category; log this operation as another new event, leaving historical entries intact. Reversal changes the Node category only: **do not revert or erase its current text, attached evidence, incoming/outgoing links, or other history**. The display name is self-reported, not a verified audit identity (D-032). Category history adds to (rather than supersedes) Node text version history (D-033/D-034); retention policy and exact presentation remain undecided.

Each PDCA must receive a stable **internal unique ID** to separate its data and prevent cross-record contamination. The previously approved ability to save an empty PDCA (D-075) refers only to a PDCA containing **zero Nodes**, not a blank name. Do not treat a name as the record's primary identity.

**Confirmed Action tracking and status lifecycle decisions (D-085–D-089):**
- **D-085 — Structured PIC/Due Date, both optional:** Each Action Node includes separately editable **PIC** and **Due Date** fields. Do not require either value to create or save an Action; a text-only Action is valid and those values can be filled/changed later. Responsibility indicates tracking ownership, not an exclusive edit permission (D-004); this requirement does not impose a PDCA-level Owner (D-073).
- **D-086 — Action-level Status, separate from Status & Result Nodes:** Each Action Node can track its current status directly using a structured field; users need not create a Status & Result Node just to reflect action progress. The **Status & Result** Node category remains available to describe actual outcomes, supporting evidence and linked PDCA reasoning. The two concepts must remain distinguishable rather than creating redundant mandatory records.
- **D-087 — Fixed five-state vocabulary:** Exactly **Not Started**, **In Progress**, **Completed**, **Canceled**, **Blocked** are the allowed Action Status values. **Canceled** means discontinued; **Blocked** means prevented from proceeding by an obstacle and could resume. They are two separate states, not the combined phrase “Canceled or Blocked”. They are not a progress percentage and Blocked is not Finished. **Initial state is Not Started (D-093)**; closed Actions may freely reopen under D-104; no other forced transition constraints have been specified. Preserve readable, consistent spelling in the UI.
- **D-088 — Completion without forced evidence:** Allow setting an Action directly to **Completed** without a required result description, upload, or any linked **Status & Result** Node. Users can add results/evidence later. This retains the freedom to revise Status and does not imply the Action is audited or independently validated as successful.
- **D-089 — Event log for Action Status changes:** Each saved status change captures **old value, new value, self-entered editor display name, and change date/time**. Authorized users can review the transitions. No separate one-click status history restoration is required; a normal status update may set a previously used value and is logged as a new event. Name is only a self-declared label and not proof of identity (D-032). Status history lifetime is confirmed (D-097); concurrency implementation remains undecided; protect the history when a Node is restored/deleted per existing data-safety rules.
**Confirmed Action follow-up, deadlines and overview (D-090–D-095):**
- **D-090 — Overdue as a calculated property, not Status:** If an Action has a Due Date before the current factory-local calendar date and its Status is **Not Started, In Progress or Blocked**, display an **Overdue** indication. If Completed or Canceled, the Action is not Overdue for current tracking, regardless of its past Due Date. Do not change the stored status, add a sixth Status or trigger any notifications. No Due Date means no Overdue.
- **D-091 — Date-only Due Date:** Action deadline has **no time component**. The Action is due until the end of its selected factory-local date and is eligible for Overdue from the start of the **next** factory-local calendar date, subject to D-090. Timezone/host-clock implementation and midnight refresh are DESIGN/test details rather than new owner choices.
- **D-092 — In-PDCA Action Overview:** Give each PDCA a focused overview/list of **its own Action Nodes** showing each Action, status, open/incomplete versus completed/canceled state, Due Date and Overdue as relevant, with suitable simple usability. This supplements the visual Node graph and does not imply a separate factory-wide Action Overview or advanced cross-project dashboard. Presentation and action counts/layout remain DESIGN details; status meanings are unchanged.
- **D-093 — Initial Action Status:** When an Action Node is created, initialize its status automatically as **Not Started**. The user may subsequently select any approved state (D-087). It is not necessary to select one to save the Action. Existing Action data must not be silently reset to Not Started after save, reload, or a later update.
- **D-094 — Single primary PIC:** Keep one optional **plain text** primary PIC field on each Action; it is not a multi-person field. Other people may be mentioned in the regular Action description. Do not require a separate contributors list or confer editing authority solely through the PIC; no PDCA project Owner is mandatory (D-073).
- **D-095 — Historical Due Date changes:** On each saved change to an Action Due Date, retain its **previous value and replacement value (including blank if set/cleared)**, self-declared editor name and change timestamp. Authorized users can view the log. **Do not require a change reason**. No distinct one-click Due Date revert feature was requested; the user may edit the date normally. Apply Node editing and Auto-save protections (D-027/D-028); record-lifetime retention is confirmed (D-097); concurrency mechanics remain open.
**Confirmed Node content, history and export policies (D-096–D-101):**
- **D-096 — Simple non-Action Nodes:** For the six Node categories other than Action, use common title/name and free-text content, with optional attached images and external document links; do not require type-specific structured fields. Action's distinct PIC, Due Date and Status rules remain.
- **D-097 — History retained for record lifetime:** Retain approved Node text revisions, Node-category changes, directed-link create/delete events, Action Status changes and Action Due Date changes for as long as the owning Node/PDCA remains retained, including seven-day recoverable Trash. No automatic 1-/3-year history expiry. On final permanent deletion remove that record's live application history. Retained backup snapshots are subject to the 30-day backup policy (D-106), with post-deletion record retention/erasure subject to IT review; no special image deletion audit is implied.
- **D-098 — Images can be added/removed without separate audit:** Authorized editors may add or remove individual Node image attachments without separately recording a per-image add/remove audit event and without a dedicated image-level restore feature; other attached images, Node content, graph edges and already confirmed histories remain intact. - **D-099 — PDF + Excel + JSON exports:** Support the already approved hybrid graph-plus-details PDF report, structured tabular Excel export, and machine-readable JSON export for a PDCA. Markdown is not mandatory in initial scope. None of these formats substitutes for the automatic full-system Backup/Restore.

- **D-101 — JSON includes current graph + retained histories; image references only:** For a single PDCA, JSON includes current PDCA identity/title, Node IDs/categories/text, Action PIC/Due Date/Status, directed edges, source-document hyperlinks and retained approved text/category/link/Action-status/Action-due-date histories. Image attachments appear only as references/metadata, not embedded binary images; the JSON may not provide accessible photos outside the host. **Confirmed final pre-audit product decisions (D-102–D-107):**

**Closure-01 — Seven shared Node semantics (approved).** Each of the seven Node types has a common **Title** and **Content**; Action additionally has optional structured PIC and Due Date and the already approved Status field. Definitions below are guidance for consistent classification, not new required metadata. Factory examples are illustrative, not claims about real incidents.

| Node type | Approved meaning | Illustrative Cutting & Packing example |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem** | An observed undesired outcome or gap against a target needing investigation/correction | A wooden box breaks during packing |
| **Fact** | Verifiable recorded information or direct observation, not guessed cause | A photo shows the Steel band offset from the wood support |
| **Assumption / Hypothesis** | An explanation or suspected causal mechanism still needing verification | Band pressure on an unsupported gap may crack the wood |
| **Action** | Work to perform and track using its own PIC, Due Date and current Status where appropriate | Inspect and correct the Steel band position on boxes being packed |
| **Status & Result** | Observable outcome, experiment result or factual progress/results of work | Inspection findings after trialing an adjusted packing method |
| **Measures to be implemented** | A preventive, control or standardization measure intended to address recurrence | Establish a standard for Steel band alignment with wooden supports |
| **Link to document** | A graph-visible reference Node representing a supporting document, potentially linked from several other Nodes | A reference to the packing work instruction (WI) |

**Distinctions:** Action is a **tracked activity**; Measures to be implemented describes a **measure or standard**. An Action may implement a Measure. Action's five-value Status is a field for work tracking, while Status & Result is an independent Node containing actual observations/results; Completed alone does not require a Result Node (D-088). A document **hyperlink on a Node** is an attached reference for that content, while a **Link to document Node** makes a source document a graph-visible object that can connect to other Nodes; actual non-image documents stay in their original systems (D-022/D-023). All seven Node types share a title and text body. **Node text search and per-PDCA Excel/JSON exports cover both Title and Content**, including Action (D-080/D-096/D-101/D-103).
